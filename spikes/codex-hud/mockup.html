Codex session HUD

B1 design preview. Illustrative data; no live connection or installed feature.

What the rows mean
Identity
Attention and the pinned session survive first; model, branch and elapsed time fit when space permits.
Usage
Request, turn and thread totals are separate measurements. Context remains unknown until checked against the native display.
Limits
Null windows mean not reported. The synthetic preset has one explicitly reported 15-minute bucket; it is not a session budget.
Full mode
Shows operating mode, observed hooks/MCP and tool completeness. A fifth row appears only with verified plan/agent records.
Geometry
Keep at least six child rows. Full collapses to compact in short panes; the HUD hides when it would leave fewer than six.

The strip sits below the child screen. Codex keeps its own composer and statusline. This preview has no keyboard interception, telemetry polling or quota inference.
